About Northfield Design

Good design.
Clear purpose.

Northfield Design creates graphic design for businesses that want to share their message clearly and look consistent wherever it appears.

The idea

Design for real business needs.

A poster needs to catch attention and communicate the details. A flyer needs to be easy to read. Social graphics need to feel like they come from the same business.

Northfield Design focuses on those practical jobs: posters, flyers, banners, social graphics and matching sets for promotions or events.

You can come with a complete brief or just an idea. We’ll work out the format, message and deadline before the design begins.
