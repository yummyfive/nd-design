About Northfield Design

Good design.
Useful support.

Northfield Design helps businesses turn the digital jobs they need done into clear, considered work they can actually use.

The idea

Creative help without the fuss.

Running a business means there is always another thing to update, create or share. A website page needs changing. An event needs a poster. A video needs editing before it can go out.

Northfield Design brings those jobs together in one place. The focus is on understanding what you need, agreeing on the details and producing work that suits your business.

Projects can be one-off, like a flyer or video, or ongoing, like keeping a website up to date or helping with social content.
